# Prism Group Live Site

PRISM GROUP — MASTER WEBSITE BUILD PROMPT

Build a world-class, premium corporate website for Prism Group, the parent company behind a portfolio of technology, AI, transformation and healthcare ventures.

This is NOT a generic consulting website.

The website should feel like a sophisticated combination of:

a premium technology company

a venture studio

an AI transformation company

an innovation group

a healthcare technology company

an enterprise technology partner

The quality bar must be significantly higher than prismdiagnostics.com.au and spectraiq.ai.

The website must feel like the parent brand that owns and connects these businesses, rather than another standalone product website.

1. CORE BRAND IDEA

The central brand concept is:

SEVEN COLOURS. ONE PRISM.

Prism Group has seven major pillars / ventures.

The Prism logo contains seven primary colour elements.

Use those seven colours as the visual DNA and navigation system of the website.

Do NOT simply create seven coloured cards.

Instead, make the seven colours feel like they are emerging from, passing through, and recombining within the Prism identity.

The visual metaphor:

One prism → seven colours → seven capabilities → one ecosystem.

The homepage should communicate that Prism takes intelligence, technology, AI and domain expertise and turns them into tangible business and healthcare outcomes.

Core brand thought:

One Prism. Seven ways to transform what's possible.

Alternative supporting language:

Building intelligent businesses for what comes next.

Operationalising intelligence. Delivering outcomes. Building what comes next.

Do not overuse these lines. Establish one primary hero statement and use the others selectively.

2. BRAND POSITIONING

Prism Group is the parent company.

It builds, owns, operates, develops and scales technology-driven businesses and solutions across:

AI transformation

AI platforms

enterprise technology

decision intelligence

mobile-first business platforms

AI voice and SMS

healthcare diagnostics

Prism should be positioned as an organisation that combines:

Domain Expertise + Data + Artificial Intelligence + Automation + Application Engineering

to create measurable commercial and operational outcomes.

The website must communicate that Prism is NOT simply:

an IT services company

an AI consultancy

a software reseller

a medical device distributor

a generic digital transformation agency

Instead:

Prism builds intelligent solutions that transform how organisations operate, decide, connect and grow.
